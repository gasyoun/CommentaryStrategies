# Sundarakāṇḍa — Critical↔Southern variant apparatus (helayo-method)

Book-wide collation of the **865 clean-variant verse pairs** between the Baroda critical edition and the southern vulgate (the text M. Leonov translates), across **66 sargas** — **2106 apparatus loci**. Gated from 1043 difflib-'variant' pairs: 104 heavily-reworded (sim < 0.8) routed to the footnote/absence layer, 4 quarantined for Cyrillic source contamination (see below). Generated by the helayo-style Gotoh aligner (`scripts/build_edition_apparatus.py`), full data in `apparatus_sundara_variants.json`.

Notation: `verse  lemma (critical) ] variant (southern)`. VARIANT layer only; whole-passage absences are in `build_edition_footnotes.py`. 2 witnesses — Center-Star MSA latent until Gita Press is the 3rd. Aligner is spike-grade (char-level + word-expansion); an akṣara-level rebuild (H776) would refine loci further.

⚠️ **Data bug surfaced by the aligner:** 4 southern verses carry Cyrillic characters mis-encoded as Sanskrit (e.g. `saṃcukoсa` with a Cyrillic `с`) — a corpus-source defect worth fixing upstream in `SamudraManthanam`. Listed in the JSON `cyrillic_contaminated`.
