## Grant — verbatim

> I, Sudalaimuthu Palaniappan, in my capacity as editor of the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa section of the Gita
> Supersite, hereby grant permission to Marcis Gasuns to reproduce, distribute, and make available the
> Sanskrit text, the seven traditional Sanskrit commentaries, and the modern English word‑by‑word glosses
> and explanations as published on https://valmiki.gitasupersite.in, for inclusion in an open‑source
> research corpus and in a Zenodo archive, under an open license, provided that the source is attributed as
> “Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, as published on the Gita Supersite (https://valmiki.gitasupersite.in), used by
> permission of the editor, Sudalaimuthu Palaniappan.” This permission is non‑exclusive, worldwide,
> perpetual, and royalty‑free. Date: 1 July 2026.

attribution string above.

## Provenance & residual-risk note

Permission was granted by Sudalaimuthu Palaniappan on 1 July 2026 in his stated capacity as **editor of
the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa section** of the Gita Supersite — i.e. an editorial-authority grant covering the
digital edition, the commentaries, and the modern English glosses. This is an editor-level grant rather
than a separate institutional (IIT Kanpur) instrument; the project relies in good faith on the editor's
representation of authority over the published section. No further clearance is required for the scope
above.
